    Syntax 
    ## Department name
Lead: free text line (optional)
> Section title (yellow card, repeat as needed)
  Optional note, indented two spaces
- Sheet name | owner | high | https://link
  Optional note, indented two spaces
- Another sheet | owner | medium
> Next section
- Sheet in the next section | owner | low

## Apps
- App name | what it does

## Links
- Sheet A -> Sheet B | IMPORTRANGE
- Sheet C -> Sheet D | manual copy

    A ">" line starts a section, and the "-" lines under it belong to it. An indented line under a section or item is its note: tap the card on the map to read it. Criticality is high, medium or low. A link whose note contains "manual" is drawn dashed. Names in Links must match a sheet or app name.
