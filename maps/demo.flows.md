# Weekly payroll
Every: Friday, then Monday
Teams: Harvest, IT, Finance
1. Harvest | Check piece work | Fri
  Count each picker's boxes against the weighbridge slips.
2. IT | Top up | Fri | <- Harvest: Piece work ready
3. Finance | Review shortage hours | Mon | sheet: Payroll Summary | <- IT: Top up done
  Headcount is copied in by hand first, so check it against HR.
4. Finance | Review OT | Mon | sheet: Headcount

# Quote approval
Every: per quote
Teams: Sales, Finance
1. Sales | Draft quote | sheet: Quotes
2. Finance | Sign off quotes over $10k | <- Sales: Quote over $10k | sheet: Invoice Log | -> 1: Changes needed
3. Sales | Send quote to customer | <- Finance: Approved
